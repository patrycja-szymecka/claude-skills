## By setting / work

- **Corporate office**: low to moderate sillage. Iris, Vetiver, Bergamot, Cedar, Lavender, White Musk.
- **Creative field**: room for something unusual. Fig, Incense, Tea, Saffron, Violet Leaf, Iso E Super.
- **Close-contact work (healthcare, teaching, hospitality)**: skin scents. Musk, Iris, Tea, light citrus. Exclude Oud, Tuberose, Patchouli and heavy gourmands.
- **Executive / formal events**: Leather, Oud, Rose, Incense, Ambergris, Sandalwood.
- **Student / casual**: Citrus, fruity, Sea Notes, light Vanilla, Ambroxan.
- **Outdoor / active**: Citrus, Mint, Sea Notes, Vetiver, Ginger.

## By age (soft guidance)

Age matters less than taste, so use it only to break ties.
- **Under 25**: brighter, sweeter, fruitier, and higher-projection scents usually go over well.
- **25–40**: balanced. A fresh option plus a warm one.
- **40–55**: refined materials such as Iris, Leather, Sandalwood and Rose. Fine to go niche.
- **55+**: classic structures such as chypre (Bergamot + Oakmoss + Patchouli) and fougère (Lavender + Coumarin + Oakmoss), or plush musks.

## By season / time

- **Spring/summer, day**: Citrus, Neroli, Green Tea, Sea Notes, Fig, Mint
- **Autumn/winter, night**: Vanilla, Amber, Tobacco, Oud, Incense, Cinnamon
- **All-year**: Iris, Musk, Vetiver, Sandalwood, Lavender

## Gender filter

Fragrantica labels perfumes "for women", "for men", or "for women and men" (unisex). Unisex results usually suit anyone. Only leave them out if the user asks.
