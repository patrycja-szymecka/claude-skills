Your portrait

Brightness with a shadow

Orange Blossom · Incense · Musk

You carry two temperatures. The first impression is warm and open: you connect easily, and people quickly feel at ease around you. But that is only the first layer. Underneath is someone with their own opinions, who doesn't explain their choices and doesn't need everyone to understand them straight away.

The scents that really suit you work the same way. They start bright and friendly, and over time reveal something deeper. You like warmth, but warmth with character. Sweetness for its own sake bores you quickly.

What moves you
  • Light that has its shadow
  • Warmth without the icing
  • Things you discover over time
  • A style of your own that needs no explaining
Your scent style

A bright opening and a sensual finish. Flowers, but never polite ones. A scent that works up close and stays in the room longer than you do.

In farewell

Anna, you don't have to say everything at once. Neither does your scent.

Pick one bottle and give it a whole day. In the morning you'll meet its bright side, in the evening the other one. The one that smells more like you than like perfume by the end of the day is yours.

P.S. If someone asks what you're wearing, you don't have to tell them right away.
